# Frequently Asked Questions

## How can I join Shrinik?
Shrinik is open for ideas and collaboration. To join or get involved, reach out via email at shrinikclub@gmail.com or DM on Instagram @shrinik_glbajaj. You can also connect with any core team member on campus.

## Who can be part of Shrinik?
Students of G.L. Bajaj Institute of Technology and Management across different skills — tech, design, editorial, PR, social media, event management, dance and music. One community, different skills, one team.

## What does Shrinik do?
It is the CSE technical club of GL Bajaj that works across technology, creativity and culture: technical events, cultural events, workshops, street theatre (Nukkad Naatak), talks (GLB Talks) and community celebrations.

## Is Shrinik only for CSE students?
No. While it is the official Computer Science & Engineering technical club of GL Bajaj, its teams span design, editorial, PR, social media, event management, dance and music — welcoming varied skills and interests.

## When was Shrinik established?
Shrinik was established in 2021.

## Where is Shrinik located?
G.L. Bajaj Institute of Technology and Management, Greater Noida.

## Does Shrinik organize cultural events too?
Yes. Beyond tech, Shrinik has Dance Avenue (Western and Semi-Classical wings) and Music Avenue, plus cultural events that bring campus together.

## How do I collaborate with Shrinik for an event or idea?
The club is open for ideas and collaboration. Email shrinikclub@gmail.com with your idea, or reach out via Instagram @shrinik_glbajaj or phone +91 97601 66210.

## What are some past events of Shrinik?
GLB Talks (flagship annual ideas event), Nukkad Naatak (street-play awareness initiative) and Farewell (send-off for graduating seniors).

## Who leads Shrinik in 2026–27?
President Vibhor Sharma, Vice President Prajjval Verma, Organizer Anushka Shukla, Secretary Siddhant Singh, Joint Secretary Kritika, and Student Coordinator Gaurang Aggarwal.
