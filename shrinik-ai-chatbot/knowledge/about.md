# About Shrinik

## What is Shrinik?
Shrinik is the official Computer Science & Engineering technical club of G.L. Bajaj Institute of Technology and Management, Greater Noida, established in 2021. It is a student-driven community where technology, creativity and culture come together — a space where students don't just participate, they create.
Taglines: "Where Ideas Become Innovation." and "Where Technology Meets Culture."

## Ideology
Technology. Creativity. Culture. Shrinik believes a student community becomes stronger when different skills are given a common purpose. It brings builders, creators, communicators, performers and organisers together so ideas move from learning to expression, and from expression to experience.

## Vision
To build a student-driven community where technology, creativity and culture come together to inspire learning, collaboration and meaningful experiences.

## Three Pillars
1. Technology — explore, experiment and build solutions that turn ideas into working experiences.
2. Creativity — give ideas an identity through design, media, editorial work and campaigns.
3. Culture — bring people together through music, dance, events and shared experiences.

## The Shrinik Way: Learn, Build, Connect
- LEARN: weekly learning sessions, workshops and hands-on experiences across Web Development, AI/ML, Competitive Programming and emerging technologies.
- BUILD: hackathons, coding challenges and real-world projects that turn concepts into practical solutions.
- CONNECT: grow with peers, seniors and industry professionals in a collaborative technical community.

## The Shrinik Experience
LEARN → CREATE → COLLABORATE → EXPERIENCE → IMPACT
From workshops and shared knowledge, through technology, design, content and performance, to events, campaigns and community moments that people remember.

## Ecosystem Areas
Tech · Design · Editorial · PR · Social Media · Events · Dance · Music
