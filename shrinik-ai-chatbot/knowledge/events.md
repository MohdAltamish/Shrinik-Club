# Events & Journey

## GLB Talks
"Ideas Worth Sharing." A flagship annual event organised by Shrinik Club, bringing people together around knowledge, ideas and meaningful conversations.

## Nukkad Naatak
"Awareness Beyond the Classroom." An initiative organised by Shrinik Club to engage college students and spread awareness through the powerful medium of street theatre.

## Farewell
"Celebrating the Journey." A heartfelt occasion organised to give the graduating seniors of G.L. Bajaj a memorable and meaningful send-off.

## Types of Activities
- Technical Events: workshops, competitions and hands-on tech experiences.
- Cultural Events: music, dance and creative campus experiences.
- Workshops: learning sessions across Web Development, AI/ML, Competitive Programming and emerging technologies.
- Community Events: celebrations, collaborations and shared experiences.

## 2026–27 Chapter
These events form part of the club's documented journey bridging Shrinik's identity with its 2026–27 chapter under the new core team.
